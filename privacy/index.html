A.C.A.S PRIVACY POLICY
Effective Date: 09/11/2026 (dd/mm/yyyy)
1. About A.C.A.S
A.C.A.S is an open-source passion project maintained by one developer. More time and money go into it than anyone gets out of it. You can find a contact link in the footer of the home page.
The home page and some documentation pages have ads and analytics, but the app has no ads and does not load Google services.
2. Your App Data
Your app settings, usage statistics and analysis stay on your device unless you choose to send analysis to a Discord webhook. The developers do not receive or store your app data. You can clear your settings in your browser or userscript manager.
If enabled, a Discord webhook sends your chosen analysis details, including a board image if selected, to your own webhook. The calculation server runs on your device and does not send data to the developers.
GitHub Pages hosts this website and may receive technical information such as your IP address. The developers, maintainers and author do not receive that information from GitHub.
3. Ads and Analytics
The website uses Google AdSense, Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics. Google collects information to provide ads and analytics, and the project may receive reports from Google, such as the total number of page views.
Google and other ad providers may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google and its partners may use advertising cookies to personalize ads. Learn more about how Google uses information from partner sites.
If you decline, the site requests non-personalized ads. Ads may still appear, and Google Analytics may still receive cookieless measurements. Accepting also allows analytics storage. Google may set or read cookies depending on your settings. Klaro stores your choice in the klaro cookie for up to 360 days.
You can change or withdraw your choice in Privacy settings. You can also opt out of personalized ads in Google Ads Settings or manage some other providers' settings at YourAdChoices.
4. Your Rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data, restrict or object to processing, request a copy, and withdraw consent. Google, GitHub and Discord handle data under their own privacy policies and may process it outside the European Economic Area.
5. Updates
This policy may change as A.C.A.S changes. The date above shows when it was last updated.
