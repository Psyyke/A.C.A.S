What to expect

                    
This learns habits, not a whole person. Our strongest checked models matched about 62% of moves on new boards, compared with about 54% for original Maia 3 on the same boards. They matched 84% on familiar opening boards, 67% in new sparse endings and 61% in middlegames. These measure move prediction. Your results will vary.

                    
What does better move matching mean?

                    
Going from 50% to 60% means the model picked your move on 60 out of 100 boards instead of 50. It may start playing more like you, especially in your usual openings. Try a few games with it and see whether its choices feel familiar.

                    
People do not always choose the same move on the same board. Mood, fatigue, time pressure and experimentation can all affect a decision, and the model does not see all that context. Near-100% agreement on unseen games may not be achievable, but we do not yet know how close we can get. Do not worry if you are far from perfect agreement.

                    
                    On a side note, I suspect move consistency is a major signal chess sites use to detect engine use. Maia 3 was built by talented researchers and imitates humans very well, yet even with personalization, exceeding 65% on new positions has been difficult in our tests.                     Engines have no emotions, so under the same conditions they tend to make the same choices.

                    
Using your model

                    
When your model is ready, download it for later.                     For Maia 3, click "Use in A.C.A.S" to apply your model directly in the app. For Maia 3 ONNX, choose Maia 3 and upload in Maia 3 Weights. For original Maia exports, choose Lc0, not Legacy Lc0, and upload in Lc0 Weights. Start with one search node to explore move imitation.

                    
Use Dynamic Settings to keep Funneling off during the opening and enable it afterward.                     DoppelMaia can learn openings especially well, since people tend to play the same openings consistently.                                         Its settings let you mix "Best" and "Any alternative" moves, for example 90% and 10%, for more varied choices. Your profile needs to provide alternative moves for this to work, so set Move Suggestion Amount (MultiPV) to at least 2.

                    
Don't forget to test your config's Elo with EloEye.

                    
Frequently asked questions

                    
Has style learning been tested?
                        
Full transformer training runs in this browser and has been checked against CUDA, including gradients, complete training runs and exported models in the app. It updates 22.4 million parameters, not just a small adapter.

                        
During development, we tested imitation using games from six Lichess accounts without BOT titles and 159,546 training positions. Each method below was evaluated on the same 21,687 boards, none used for that account's training, validation or earlier development checks.

The gain over previous personalized models was 2.54 percentage points, with a game based 95% interval from 2.05 to 3.00. All six accounts improved, but this is still not nearly perfect imitation. Two earlier accounts had too few later games and were not replaced.

                        
These development tests used larger imports and chose between two base habit settings on validation. Your run uses the settings you choose. Raise Position limit in import settings to include more of your games.

                        
Some earlier development results included BOT accounts: five of six in full model tests and nine of twenty in adapter tests. Those averages do not describe human players alone. Testing details and corrections.

                        
We explored 50 personality candidates and kept 18. Each has a distinct measured policy at the same Elo and kept its preference in fresh autonomous games. The confirmation played 552 games, including paired base controls. Extreme styles often played worse, so these are preferences rather than strength ratings. How do I personalize Maia or train Lc0?
Import your games, select your player and a supported starting network, then train. Load your new weights in A.C.A.S. Check predictions on separate games before drawing conclusions about your engine's personality.

                    
Can I make the engine play like me?
The trainer learns patterns in the games you provide. Results on new positions depend on your games and the validation results. This does not prove that the engine copies a person or plays at a particular strength.

                    
It stops when I leave the tab
Keep DoppelMaia open and stay on this tab for the entire training run. Do not switch tabs or leave the page until training is finished.

                    
What is an engine personality?
Here, personality means patterns in your choices, such as the moves you prefer. DoppelMaia does not guarantee a human playing style or a specific opening.

                    
Personality presets describe soft preferences and tradeoffs such as piece choices, captures, or king safety. They are not claims that a preset has been proven to play near perfectly.

                    

                    
Can I train any Lc0 model?
No. Custom Lc0 weights need classical inputs and a compatible convolutional policy head. If you are unsure, choose one of the included Maia networks.
