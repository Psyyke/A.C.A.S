About

                    
Converts Lc0 weights with Lc0's own leela2onnx tool. Files stay in your browser.

                    
Large networks need plenty of memory. Float16 weights make a smaller download with Float32 math, not faster GPU math.

                    
Both Float16 options can change evaluations and policy outputs. Use Float32 when accuracy matters.

                    
Uses a modified Lc0 v0.32.1, GPLv3 or later. Upstream source, browser source and build scripts, license and notices.
