How to use

Test your configured GUI profile against Maia 3, or play yourself. The result is an estimate against Maia, not an official human Elo rating.

  1. Open the A.C.A.S GUI and turn on the profiles you want to test. Use the same website address for both pages.
  2. Choose Config to play full move suggestions automatically, or Me to play yourself with hints.
  3. Keep both pages visible side by side and wait for the GUI active tick. The GUI needs the userscript, but EloEye itself does not.
  4. Choose your board count and press Start. Fewer boards are easier on your device. The first run downloads about 45 MB. Resuming finishes the saved group before switching to your new board count.
  5. Leave both windows open until your result appears. If interrupted, return and resume with the same A.C.A.S setup.

How to use the suggestion mix

Change one percentage and the others adjust for you. A few examples:

  • 90% best, 10% alternatives: about 9 out of 10 picks use a top suggestion. The rest use another shown move, including possible mistakes or blunders.
  • 80% best, 20% mistakes: turn on Choose alternative types to set this. No mistakes shown? Best gets every pick.
  • 100% mistakes, but none shown: EloEye uses another shown move and warns you. It never invents moves. A 0% type is skipped unless nothing matches.

Safe secondary is a non-top move rated close to best, with no warning. Other / unclassified means we cannot call it safe. Engine labels are not guarantees.

Profiles share one pool after they finish. Duplicate moves count once. Target-square-only hints do not block full moves. If labels disagree, the weaker one wins. Use time-limited searches and keep the same setup when resuming.

How many games?

More games usually narrow the estimate. For example, 1500 ±100 means roughly 1400 to 1600. Here is a rough idea of how many games that takes, not a guarantee.

EloEye aims for ±25 and stops at 1000 games, even if the range is still wider. Maia opponents use 10-point rating steps, and the result shows whole Elo points. Extra digits do not mean extra certainty. These counts come from simulated results against similarly strong opponents. Real runs vary. More boards save time, not games. This compares your setup with Maia, not an official human Elo rating.

One Maia worker per board can help boards run faster on some devices, but uses much more memory. It loads up to ten copies of Maia instead of sharing one. Leave it off if your device slows down. This changes performance, not the opponent or scoring.
